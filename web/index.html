FRLG Link Bridge

Trade and battle between a real Game Boy Advance and Pokémon FireRed / LeafGreen on Nintendo Switch. This page sets up the two boards that make it possible, and can carry the link between them.

1

ESP32 board

Joins the Switch's wireless room. ESP32-S3, C6, C3 or the original ESP32.

The Switch encrypts its local wireless with keys from the console, so the board needs four of them from your own Switch. The file is read here in your browser and only those four values go to the board, over USB. Nothing is uploaded.

2

GB-Link adapter

Plugs into the Game Boy Advance in place of a Wireless Adapter.

  1. Put the adapter in update mode
  2. Let the browser use it: click the button and pick RP2 Boot
  3. Write the firmware
  4. Reconnect

On the consoles
  1. On the Switch, go upstairs in a Pokémon Center to the Direct Corner, choose Trade Center or Colosseum, and become the group leader.
  2. On the Game Boy Advance, with the adapter plugged in, choose the same activity and join a group. The Switch player is listed after a few seconds.
  3. When you leave the room, the ESP32 board restarts and is ready for the next one about ten seconds later.

Trades and single battles work. Trading without a Game Boy Advance (the page standing in for the GBA) is not part of this page yet; the desktop app in host/ still does that.
